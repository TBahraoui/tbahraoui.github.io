Overview

I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics and Statistics from Université de Sherbrooke. My work focuses on mathematical statistics, specifically the development of semiparametric and nonparametric testing methodologies, multivariate dependence structures, and limit theorems for complex stochastic processes. I regularly publish in leading international statistics journals including the Journal of Multivariate Analysis and the Scandinavian Journal of Statistics.

Research Focus Areas

Copulas & Dependence

Multivariate copula exchangeability, Bernstein copulas, radial symmetry tests, and directional asymmetry modeling.

Extreme Value Theory

Tail copula characteristic functions (TCCF), extremal dependence indices, and asymptotic properties of extreme joint events.

Time Series & ML

Nonparametric change-point detection in dependent multivariate time series using pseudo-observations and rank statistics.

Empirical Process Theory

Degenerate U- and V-statistics, functional central limit theorems, and multiplier bootstrap validation for weak dependence.
