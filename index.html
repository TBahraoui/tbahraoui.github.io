--- layout: single author_profile: true permalink: / --- # About Me I am a researcher with a Ph.D. in Mathematics / Statistics from **Université de Sherbrooke**, Québec, Canada. My research lies at the intersection of statistical theory, dependence modeling, and data science, focusing on developing robust nonparametric and semiparametric inference techniques for complex multivariate data. --- ### Research Interests * **Dependence Modeling & Copulas:** Multivariate copula theory, Bernstein copulas, symmetry testing, and rank statistics. * **Extreme Value Theory:** Tail dependence, tail copula characteristic functions (TCCF), and directional asymmetry in extreme events. * **Empirical Process Theory:** Functional central limit theorems, degenerate $U$- and $V$-statistics, and bootstrap consistency under weak dependence. * **Time Series & Machine Learning:** Change-point detection in multivariate time series using pseudo-observations and nonparametric methods. --- ### Selected Publications * **Change-point problems for multivariate time series using pseudo-observations** B.
