Overview

I am a mathematical statistician specializing in dependence modeling, nonparametric inference, and multivariate analysis. My research combines theoretical foundations—including copula theory, empirical processes, and characteristic functions—with practical statistical methodologies for biostatistics, statistical genetics, and public health surveillance. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at Université de Sherbrooke with the distinction Excellent.

Research Experience

Postdoctoral Researcher 2021 – 2022
Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) · Montréal, QC
  • Engineered semiparametric binary regression models using spectral copula structures to model complex gene-gene interactions in high-dimensional genomic studies.
  • Collaborated with Prof. Karim Oualkacha and Prof. Taoufik Bouezmarni to bridge statistical dependence theory with computational biostatistics.
Postdoctoral Researcher 2020 – 2021
Université de Sherbrooke · Sherbrooke, QC
  • Established the asymptotic theory for nonparametric change-point tests in multivariate time series using pseudo-observations (in collaboration with Prof. Bruno N. Rémillard, HEC Montréal).
  • Designed multivariate sequential monitoring tools with direct applications to epidemiological surveillance and public health monitoring.
FAPESP Postdoctoral Fellow 2018 – 2019
University of São Paulo · São Paulo, Brazil
  • Developed a new class of bivariate asymmetry measures using copula characteristic functions alongside Prof. Nikolai Kolev.
  • Secured competitive international post-doctoral research funding through the São Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP).
