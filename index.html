Overview

I am a mathematical statistician with a Ph.D. in Mathematics (graduated with Distinction: Excellent) from Université de Sherbrooke. My work advances theoretical dependence modeling, empirical process theory, and nonparametric inference while bridging rigorous mathematical foundations with practical applications in biostatistics, genomic interaction analysis, and epidemiological surveillance.

Research Appointments

Postdoctoral Researcher 2021 – 2022
Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM) · Montréal, QC
  • Developed semiparametric binary regression models using spectral copula structures to analyze gene-gene interactions in high-dimensional genomic studies.
  • Collaborated with Prof. Karim Oualkacha and Prof. Taoufik Bouezmarni on public health biostatistics.
Postdoctoral Researcher 2020 – 2021
Université de Sherbrooke · Sherbrooke, QC
  • Established asymptotic theory for nonparametric change-point tests using pseudo-observations under the mentorship of Prof. Bruno N. Rémillard (HEC Montréal).
  • Designed multivariate monitoring algorithms directly applicable to epidemiological health surveillance systems.
FAPESP Postdoctoral Fellow 2018 – 2019
University of São Paulo · São Paulo, Brazil
  • Formulated novel asymmetry measures for bivariate distributions based on characteristic functions with Prof. Nikolai Kolev.
  • Secured competitive international research funding through the FAPESP fellowship.
